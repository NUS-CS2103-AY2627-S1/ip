---
name: seedu-git-standard
description: Apply the SE-EDU Git commit-message conventions when proposing or creating commits in this project.
---

Write an imperative, capitalized subject with no period; keep it near 50 characters and never over 72. For non-trivial commits, separate the subject from a body with a blank line, wrap body lines at 72 characters, and explain what changed and why (not how). Use separate commits for unrelated changes. Refer to https://se-education.org/guides/conventions/git.html for the complete standard.
