# SE-EDU Git conventions

Apply these rules whenever creating, proposing, or reviewing a commit or branch:

- Write a meaningful imperative commit subject, capitalize its first letter, omit the final period, and keep it preferably within 50 characters and never over 72 characters. An optional scope or category may precede the subject.
- For every non-trivial commit, separate the subject from a body with a blank line. Wrap body lines at 72 characters, use paragraphs or bullets where useful, and explain what changed and why; the diff explains how.
- Structure substantial commit bodies around the present situation, why it needs to change, what to do, and why that approach is appropriate. Avoid redundant commentary and words such as "currently" or "originally".
- Use meaningful kebab-case branch names, or `<issue-number>-<keywords>` when the branch relates to an issue.

Authoritative reference: https://se-education.org/guides/conventions/git.html
